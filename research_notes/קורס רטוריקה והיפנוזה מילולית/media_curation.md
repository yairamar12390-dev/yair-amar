# Curated Learning Media: Public Speaking, Rhetoric, Charisma, Storytelling, Ericksonian Language & Metaphor (EN + HE)

Research date: 2026-11-16. Verification method: web search results that return the exact URL (marked **[V]**). Direct fetching of youtube.com was blocked in this research environment (DNS/proxy policy), so YouTube links are verified only through search-index hits, not by loading the page. Items marked **[U]** are from prior knowledge and the URL was NOT confirmed in this session; check them before publishing. Levels: B = beginner, I = intermediate, A = advanced.

## YouTube videos and talks (EN + HE)

### Takeaway
A core canon of verified video items exists for each topic: Winston (MIT), the TED structure/voice/storytelling talks, Vonnegut on story shape, Lakoff on metaphor, Boroditsky on language and thought, and original Erickson footage on the Erickson Foundation channel. Hebrew YouTube content is mostly practitioner tips videos; Sivan Rahav-Meir's talk on speeches is the strongest Hebrew item found.

### Cited Findings

**Public speaking: foundations**
- [B] **"How to Speak"**, Patrick Winston (MIT OCW RES.TLL-005, IAP 2018), EN, about 1 hr. MIT tradition for 40+ years; heuristics for time, place, boards, props, slides, informing vs persuading. Best single lecture to open a course. — [V] [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Unzc731iCUY); [MIT OCW playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP60eUvXa1jfVT9BsBsqAICr_)
- [B] **"How to speak so that people want to listen"**, Julian Treasure (TED), EN, 9:44. Vocal tools (register, timbre, prosody, pace) plus the "seven deadly sins" of speaking; ready-made vocal warm-up. — [V] [TED-Ed lesson page](https://ed.ted.com/lessons/0A3PRwvK); [TED podcast transcript](https://www.ted.com/podcasts/julian-treasure-transcript). Main TED page [U]: https://www.ted.com/talks/julian_treasure_how_to_speak_so_that_people_want_to_listen
- [B] **"TED's secret to great public speaking"**, Chris Anderson (TED), EN, about 8 min. One idea, carefully built in the listener's mind. — TED curator talk confirmed on [TED presentation playlist](https://www.ted.com/playlists/574/how_to_make_a_great_presentation) [V]; direct URL [U]: https://www.ted.com/talks/chris_anderson_ted_s_secret_to_great_public_speaking
- [I] **"The secret structure of great talks"**, Nancy Duarte (TEDxEast), EN, 18 min. The "what is / what could be" contour of great speeches (MLK, Jobs); bridges speaking and storytelling. — [V] listed on [TED presentation playlist](https://www.ted.com/playlists/574/how_to_make_a_great_presentation); direct URL [U]: https://www.ted.com/talks/nancy_duarte_the_secret_structure_of_great_talks
- [B] **TED playlists "How to make a great presentation" and "Before public speaking"**, curated TED sets, EN. A ready module of short talks. — [V] [Playlist 574](https://www.ted.com/playlists/574/how_to_make_a_great_presentation); [Playlist 226](https://www.ted.com/playlists/226/before_public_speaking)
- [B] **"Think Fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques"**, Matt Abrahams (Stanford GSB), EN, about 58 min. Spontaneous speaking, anxiety management, structures (Problem-Solution-Benefit, What-So What-Now What). — [U] https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HAnw168huqA (not returned by search; check it)
- [I] **"How Great Leaders Inspire Action"**, Simon Sinek (TEDxPuget Sound, 2009), EN, about 18 min, roughly 64.7M views on TED. A classic persuasive frame ("Start with why"); good to analyze as rhetoric. — [V] [TED](https://www.ted.com/talks/simon_sinek_how_great_leaders_inspire_action)

## Podcasts (shows and specific episodes)

### Takeaway
The strongest podcast picks are Stanford's Think Fast, Talk Smart, Hidden Brain's two-part Cialdini "Persuasion" series and related episodes, and Huberman Lab's David Spiegel hypnosis episode, which covers the science of hypnosis. Verified Hebrew podcasts on rhetoric are scarce.

## Free online courses

### Takeaway
The UW Coursera course "Introduction to Public Speaking" (part of the Dynamic Public Speaking specialization) and Harvard's edX "Rhetoric" course are verified, free to audit, and form the course's academic spine. No Campus IL (קמפוס IL) or Open University free course on rhetoric was found.

### Cited Findings
- [B] **Introduction to Public Speaking**, Dr. Matt McGarrity (University of Washington), Coursera, EN. Informative, impromptu and persuasive speaking with practice; about 400K enrolled; beginner. Free to audit, paid certificate. — [V] [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/public-speaking)
- [I] **Dynamic Public Speaking Specialization** (4 courses, UW), Coursera, EN. Continues into informative, persuasive and ceremonial speaking. — [V] [Coursera](https://coursera.org/specializations/public-speaking)
- [B] **UWashingtonX COMM220x "Public Speaking"** (edX archive, 2015 run), EN. An older edX version of McGarrity's material. — [V] [edX courseware](https://courses.edx.org/courses/course-v1:UWashingtonX+COMM220UWx_2+1T2015/f9458f0bc1ae4e2f90a23281f0b7c1a2) (may require login or be archived)
- [I] **Rhetoric: The Art of Persuasive Writing and Public Speaking**, James Engell (Harvard), edX / Harvard Online, EN, 8 weeks at 2-3 hrs/wk. Classical-to-modern rhetoric analysis (Lincoln, Churchill, MLK), fallacies, op-ed and speech writing; newest edition covers social media and generative AI. Free audit; certificate about $209. — [V] [Harvard Online](https://www.harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/rhetoric-art-persuasive-writing-public-speaking); [Harvard PLL](https://pll.harvard.edu/node/23859)
- [B] **MIT OCW RES.TLL-005 "How to Speak"**, free, EN. Winston's lecture as a formal OCW resource (see YouTube). — [V] [Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP60eUvXa1jfVT9BsBsqAICr_)
- [B] **Class Central "Best Public Speaking Courses" roundup**, EN. Discovery aid for further free MOOCs. — [V] [Class Central](https://classcentral.com/report/?p=85906)
- Israeli options (paid or academic, not free): Reichman University's FORE-RUNI "תכנית לאמנות הרטוריקה" and "רטוריקה 360", HE. In-person speaking in front of an audience and camera. — [V] [Rhetoric 360 page](https://www.fore-runi.com/%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94/); Tel Aviv University syllabus "רטוריקה והמסר התקשורתי" (Dr. Yaron Cohen-Tsemach), useful as a reading-list model — [V] [Syllabus PDF](https://www30.tau.ac.il/InternetFiles/yedion/syllabus/06/2019/0662/0662103101_%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94%20%D7%95%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A8%20%20%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%AA%D7%99_syl.pdf); Bar-Ilan "רטוריקה" course reader — [V] [PDF](https://desigprog.biu.ac.il/files/michlalot/rtvryqh.pdf)

- With no free Hebrew MOOC available, the Bar-Ilan reader and the TAU syllabus are the best free Hebrew academic texts to adapt.

## Essential books (EN + HE)

### Takeaway
The English canon is well established. Hebrew rhetoric books exist (Aristotle in Tzoran's translation, Magnes "The Art of Persuasion", Gati, Kayam), but Hebrew sources for Ericksonian language are thin. Book entries mostly lack verified purchase links, so treat the bibliographic data as standard but unverified this session unless marked [V].

### Cited Findings
**Public speaking and TED (B to I)**
- [B] *TED Talks: The Official TED Guide to Public Speaking*, Chris Anderson (2016). Throughline, connection, explanation, persuasion. — [U]
- [B] *Talk Like TED*, Carmine Gallo (2014). Nine "secrets" distilled from top TED talks. — [U]
- [B] *Think Faster, Talk Smarter*, Matt Abrahams (2023). The book version of the Stanford course. — [U]
- [I] *Resonate*, Nancy Duarte (2010; free online version historically at duarte.com). The "sparkline" story structure. — [U]

**Rhetoric (I to A)**
- [I] *Thank You for Arguing*, Jay Heinrichs (4th ed. 2020). Aristotle and Cicero made practical. — [U]
- [A] Aristotle, *Rhetoric* / *רטוריקה*, Hebrew translation by Gabriel Tzoran, HE. The source text for ethos, pathos, logos. — cited as a recommended Hebrew source by [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%9F) [V-citation]
- [I] Shai Pragai, *רטוריקה* (Devir, 2006), HE. — cited by [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%9F); not independently verified (a follow-up search found no Devir listing)
- [I] Yehoshua Gati, *אמנות הרטוריקה* (Open University, 2013), HE. — cited by [Hamichlol](https://www.hamichlol.org.il/%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%9F); not independently verified
- [I] Yehoshua Gati & Dolly Levy, *שלושה יוצרים נמר – רטוריקת הפופוליזם* (Asif), HE. Rhetorical analysis of Israeli political speech. — [V] [Maariv review](https://www.maariv.co.il/culture/literature/Article-707119)
- [I] *אמנות השכנוע*, Magnes Press (1993, 244 pp.), HE. Persuasion from Greece and Rome to the modern speaker and audience. — [V] [Magnes](https://www.magnespress.co.il/en/book/אמנות_השכנוע-284)
- [A] Orly Kayam, *רטוריקה על כף המאזניים*, HE. Rhetoric of Israeli Supreme Court rulings. — [V] [PDF](https://www.wincol.ac.il/wincol.ac.il/originals/Kayam.pdf)
- [A] Avraham Melamed, "הרטוריקה העברית" (article), HE. Hebrew and Jewish rhetorical tradition from the Bible onward. — [V] [Mikan, BGU](https://mikan.bgu.ac.il/index.php/mikan/article/view/839)
- [A] *Performing Persuasion: Aristotle and Talmud* (Hebrew University publication), HE. — [V] [HUJI](https://politics.huji.ac.il/publications/performing-persuasion-two-rhetorical-approaches-aristotle-and-talmud-hebrew)

**Charisma and storytelling (B to I)**
- [B] *The Charisma Myth*, Olivia Fox Cabane (2012). Presence, power and warmth as trainable skills. — [U]
- [B] *Made to Stick*, Chip & Dan Heath (2007). SUCCESs model (simple, unexpected, concrete, credible, emotional, stories). — [U]
- [I] *Influence* (new and expanded 2021), Robert Cialdini. Pairs with the Hidden Brain episodes. — [U]

**Metaphor and language patterns (I to A)**
- [I] *Metaphors We Live By*, George Lakoff & Mark Johnson (1980). Conceptual metaphor; the theoretical basis for the metaphor module. — book confirmed in [Lakoff video description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puiMnXZD2w0) [V-citation]
- [I] *Don't Think of an Elephant!*, George Lakoff (2004). Framing in practice. — [U]
- [I] *Words That Change Minds*, Shelle Rose Charvet (3rd ed. 2019). LAB Profile language patterns. — [U]

**Ericksonian / conversational hypnosis (A)**
- [A] *Patterns of the Hypnotic Techniques of Milton H. Erickson, M.D.*, Vol. 1, Richard Bandler & John Grinder (1975). The "Milton Model" language patterns. — [U]
- [A] *My Voice Will Go With You: The Teaching Tales of Milton H. Erickson*, Sidney Rosen (1982). Erickson's teaching stories, a model for therapeutic metaphor. — [U]
- [A] *Hypnotic Realities*, Erickson, Rossi & Rossi (1976). Erickson's own analysis of indirect suggestion. — [U]
- [A] *Therapeutic Trances*, Stephen Gilligan (1987). The standard textbook on Ericksonian practice. — [U]
- [A] *Experiencing Erickson* / *The Induction of Hypnosis*, Jeffrey Zeig. — [U]
- [A] *Metaphor in Psychotherapy* (Kopp) or *Therapeutic Metaphors* (David Gordon, 1978). Building metaphors. — [U]

- For the hypnosis module, books plus Erickson Foundation footage are the main resources, since free lecture-quality video is limited.

- No Hebrew book on Ericksonian hypnosis or conversational hypnosis was found.
